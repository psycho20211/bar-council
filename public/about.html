About Our Association

Discover our history, mission, and the values that have guided us for over four decades of service to the professional community.

Our Story

Founded in 1985, the National Professional Association of Engineers & Specialists began with a small group of dedicated professionals who shared a vision of unity, excellence, and mutual support. What started as a local initiative has grown into one of the most respected professional bodies in the nation.

Today, with over 5,000 active members and 12 specialized subcommittees, we continue to uphold the founding principles while embracing innovation and modern practices to better serve our community.

Our Mission

To empower professionals through advocacy, education, and collective action, creating an environment where every member can thrive and contribute to the advancement of our field.

Our Vision

To be the leading professional association recognized nationally and internationally for excellence, innovation, and the unwavering commitment to our members' growth and well-being.

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, inclusivity, and excellence guide everything we do. We believe in the power of community and the responsibility we hold toward each member.
